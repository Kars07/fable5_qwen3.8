### 1. 🎯 Distinct Reward Semantics: Regime 1 vs. Regime 2
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

While sharing a unified three-component reward structure (Structure, Correctness, Submission Integrity),
Regime 1 and Regime 2 interpret "Correctness" differently based on their operational formulation:

### 2. 🛡️ Correctness-Gated Anti-Gaming Reward Architecture
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

In Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO), advantage is normalized across group rollouts (G=4):
  A_i = (R_i - μ_G) / (σ_G + ε)

Therefore:
  A_i < 0  iff  R_i < μ_G

The -0.50 submission penalty does not rely on absolute negativity; rather, it ensures that a premature
or fake completion claim receives a score (e.g. -0.30) that falls substantially below the group mean μ_G.
Whenever its reward lies below μ_G, GRPO assigns it a negative relative advantage, actively suppressing
that behavior. Under normal within-group diversity (0% zero-variance groups), this makes A_i < 0 overwhelmingly
likely for unearned completion claims.

### 3. 🔍 Policy Token Masking Specification
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

In multi-turn interactive execution, the sequence structure contains interleaved policy actions and
external environment observations:

  SYSTEM PROMPT             mask = 0
  USER TASK DESCRIPTION     mask = 0

  ASSISTANT TURN 1          mask = 1  (sampled policy action)
  TERMINAL OBSERVATION 1    mask = 0  (external environment context)

  ASSISTANT TURN 2          mask = 1  (sampled policy action)
  TERMINAL OBSERVATION 2    mask = 0  (external environment context)

  ...

  ASSISTANT TURN T (<= 6)   mask = 1  (sampled policy action)

The policy gradient loss is computed strictly over the generated assistant tokens:
  T_policy = ⋃_{t=1}^T (assistant tokens_t)

This ensures terminal stdout/stderr observations are never penalized or reinforced as if they were
sampled tokens from the policy.

### 4. 📊 GRPO Group Variance (G=4) & Context Budget Audit (16K)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. GRPO Within-Group Variance Diagnostics:
   When all G=4 candidates receive identical reward (σ_R = 0, e.g. [0,0,0,0] or [1,1,1,1]), the group
   contributes zero learning signal. We monitor:
   - Zero-variance group percentage (Target: < 40%)
   - All-zero and all-perfect group saturation rates
   - Mean reward (μ) and standard deviation (σ) within batch
   - Premature Submission Penalty Rate: tracking instances of unearned completion claims

2. Context Budget Breakdown (16,384 tokens):
   L_total = L_system + L_task + ∑_{t=1}^T (L_assistant,t + L_observation,t) <= 16,384
   - Truncation rate monitored continuously via inspect_rl_outputs.py (p50, p90, p95, p99 quantiles).
   - With T_max = 6, trajectory credit assignment is deliberately bounded; current reward-variance
     diagnostics indicate GRPO is operating stably at this horizon.
